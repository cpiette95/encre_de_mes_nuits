The Silence of Our Kiss

July 15, 2026
Silence. No touch, no words. A walk of silence beneath the smothering dusk, carrying unspoken words, like strangled strings— no breath coming in. No broken strings. Only a knot too tight. A tiny whisper. Mouth-to-mouth. Again— The strings vibrate. Laughter and smiles escape. Let them be loud, louder, louder still so the world hears the silence of our kiss.
· · ·

Context & Notes

When an argument with A. on the loudness of an outdoor conversation inspires these words of reconciliation.
